# Tank&Tap - Gas & Water Delivery Scheduler

A mobile-friendly web app designed to eliminate sudden utility stockouts for Filipino households and micro-businesses through instant QR reordering.

> Note: This repository contains the **design, research, and product planning** for Tank&Tap.
> This project was developed as part of a system design and technopreneurship requirement and is being considered for further development in the future.
> As such, detailed implementation code and full business strategies are not publicly disclosed.

---

## Project Overview

- **Goal:** Eliminate local supply blockages and delivery anxiety through friction-free ordering utilities
- **Focus:** User accessibility, local logistics optimization, and operational efficiency
- **Output:** Figma prototype, system process mapping, business model, and project scope frameworks

## Problem

LPG and water refilling operations are heavily crisis-driven; users only order after a total supply failure hits, producing deep delivery anxiety due to absent supplier ETAs. Concurrently, micro-retailers suffer from severe administrative strain—manually sorting chaotic text logs by street proximity. Because of tight operational margins, merchants heavily resist adopting premium, high-barrier digital platforms.

---

## Solution

The solution focused on speed and simplicity:

- **Frictionless Mobile Ordering:** Replaces slow, crisis-driven reordering with instant mobile flows that require zero data entry or complex account setups.
- **Smart Route Optimization:** Eliminates manual booking chaos for local micro-retailers by automating delivery tracking and dispatch workflows.
- **Inclusive Onboarding Strategy:** Lowers digital resistance for non-tech-savvy users and small neighborhood shops by offering an instantly usable system interface.
- **Risk-Free Monetization:** Removes upfront software subscriptions for thin-margin merchants, funding operations through minor consumer platform convenience fees.

## Key Features

- **One-Tap QR Re-Order Stickers**: Instantly routes users to custom checkout screens with zero baseline data entry.
- **Proximity Zoning Dashboard**: Automatically batches and maps incoming customer orders by street to optimize delivery routes.
- **Service Capability Toggles**: Provides quick directory filters to find backup merchants offering setup support or heavy lifting.
- **COD Invoice Integration**: Seamlessly appends platform processing fees right onto cash-on-delivery receipts.
- **Low-Barrier Onboarding**: Removes mandatory account setups and registration forms to accommodate non-tech-savvy users.

## Challenges

The biggest barriers to building this concept.

- Low trust in new digital services for urgent utility needs.
- Resistance from small merchants due to cost and complexity.
- Difficulty balancing convenience with operational simplicity.

---

## What I Learned

Key lessons from shaping the product concept.

- Simple products win trust faster than complex ones.
- Good onboarding matters as much as the core feature.
- Operational reality should guide product design from the start.
